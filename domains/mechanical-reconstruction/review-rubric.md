# Mechanical Reconstruction — Review Rubric
Version: 0.1.0 · Proposed release policy, separate from manufacturing certification.

| Criterion | Weight | Full-credit evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Source/view interpretation | 20 | Projection and source-linked dimensions resolved |
| Feature plan and geometry fidelity | 20 | Valid DAG, exact required profiles and intended dependencies |
| Dimensions and holes | 25 | Independent final measurements meet approved tolerances |
| Solid topology | 15 | Expected valid bodies, cavities and required treatments verified |
| Neutral export and recovery | 20 | Reopened exchange artifact plus failure/replay evidence |

Rate 0–4: 0 absent/failed; 1 major gaps; 2 partial; 3 meets scope; 4 complete including adverse-case evidence. Score = sum(weight × rating/4).

Release requires ≥95/100 and all hard gates: no unresolved critical view/dimension ambiguity; acyclic valid feature plan; required exactness; independently measured holes/dimensions; valid expected topology; actual public MCP route; required neutral artifact reopened; recovery verified; sealed hashes and applicable standards resolved for any compliance claim.

A visually plausible or single-valid-solid result cannot override a failed measurement gate. An independent reviewer records method, evidence and critical finding disposition. Any approved approximation is labeled in deliverables; it cannot be called exact/manufacturing-ready reconstruction.
