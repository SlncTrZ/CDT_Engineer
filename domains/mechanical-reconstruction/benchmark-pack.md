## Oracle and release
A responsible reviewer freezes source-linked dimensions, tolerances, expected topology and required exchange format before generation. Exact reconstruction cannot pass with unapproved sampled radii. Generic solid validity, bbox, volume and screenshots do not establish hole correctness.

Record exact native application, backend/build/public contract, pack versions, tool receipts, independent inspector, output hashes and unresolved findings. Required export unavailable means blocked, not native-only PASS. Final evidence binds the sealed artifacts and must pass the domain review rubric.
